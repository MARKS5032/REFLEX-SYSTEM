## How to use this log

1. **Time the full run** — from opening the slides to the closing line of
   the demo script — with a stopwatch, not an estimate.
2. **Note what went well** — a slide that landed, a demo step that was
   smooth, a question you handled confidently.
3. **Note what was too long** — a slide you over-explained, a demo step that
   stalled (e.g. waiting on a page load), a tangent you went on.
4. **Record what you changed** before the next run — cut a slide, shorten a
   sentence, reorder a demo step, rehearse a transition.

## Suggested target

- Executive presentation: ~3 minutes (5 slides, ~35 seconds each)
- Live demo: ~4-6 minutes (see `demo-script.md`)
- Total: under 10 minutes, leaving room for panel questions
